## 2024-05-15 - Enhancing Icon-Only Button Accessibility and Interactions
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons frequently suffer from inadequate accessibility (missing `aria-label`, `title` for tooltips, and keyboard `focus-visible` states). Additionally, dynamic icon-only buttons that conditionally display text (e.g., Download/Copy buttons displaying 'Downloaded' briefly) experience jarring layout shifts if padding and aspect ratios are not carefully constrained when in the icon-only state.
**Action:** When creating or reviewing icon-only buttons, always ensure a `title` tooltip, an `aria-label` (or screen reader equivalent), and a distinct `focus-visible:ring-2` outline are present. For buttons that transition to text, constrain width and padding (e.g., `w-10 px-0` or `w-11`) during the icon-only state to prevent layout jank upon interaction.
